The Last Tenant

A short film in a building that remembers.

Move your cursor — it’s dark in here.

The logline

When a greedy landlord traps an elderly tenant inside her mold-infested apartment, his cruelty unleashes a sentient fungal horror that transforms her into a vengeful monster, forcing him to confront his sins before he becomes the building’s last tenant.

The infestation

Months later, a mysterious infestation spreads through the building, revealing Evelyn’s horrifying transformation. As the mold consumes everything, Walter faces the consequences of his cruelty and becomes the building’s last tenant.

Who lives here

Hover or tap a card. Evelyn changes when the lamp finds her.

Evelyn Vale (Mrs. Cat)

An elderly cat tenant with cream-colored fur, a faded floral dress, and a cardigan. Gentle and vulnerable until her abandonment leads to a monstrous transformation.

Walter Harlan

A greedy, middle-aged rat landlord wearing a brown tweed jacket and red tie. Cold, dismissive, and obsessed with rent payments.

Jean

A young dog tenant wearing round glasses and a green cardigan. Kind, observant, and increasingly terrified by the infestation.

Evelyn (the mold version)

A sentient black-green fungal infestation spreading through the building. It possesses Evelyn and transforms her into a grotesque creature.

The Priest

An anthropomorphic pig in clerical clothing, summoned by Walter to confront the supernatural infestation.
